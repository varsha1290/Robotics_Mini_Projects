| LED ON/OFF operation | Not tested |
| Observed interval | Not measured |
| Comments | To be recorded |

## Procedure
1. Disconnect USB and inspect wiring.
2. Confirm resistor is in series with the LED.
3. Confirm LED polarity and GND.
4. Upload the sketch.
5. Observe at least ten cycles.
6. Record actual observations. Do not mark PASS unless physically tested.
